01 / WORK SAMPLE

A new starter should be ready—not overprivileged

Scenario: a fictional organisation is onboarding a remote operations coordinator. The manager has approved the role, start date, equipment and required systems. This sample does not include real employee data.

Access decision matrix

ResourceAccess / approvalVerification
Company laptopStandard user / ITAsset assigned; encryption and endpoint status checked
Email & collaborationStandard licence / managerWork account and mailbox tested
Operations workspaceTeam membership / ownerRequired folder opens; unrelated area remains restricted
Finance workspaceNo access requestedNo finance membership or direct share
Administrative rolesNone by defaultNo privileged role assigned

Before the start date

  1. Obtain an approved request from HR/manager through the official workflow. Verify identity and start date without relying on an unsolicited email.
  2. Record device ownership, serial/asset identifier and assigned custodian in the restricted asset system.
  3. Prepare the supported operating system, updates, approved applications, endpoint protection, encryption and management enrolment as required by the organisation.
  4. Create the account and group memberships from the approved role. Assign only the licences and permissions needed for work.
  5. Prepare a secure credential-delivery process, user-led MFA registration and recovery guidance. Keep secrets and recovery codes out of ordinary tickets.

Day-one acceptance

Verify sign-in, email, approved shared resources, remote access if required, device health and a user-completed task. Explain the support channel, phishing reporting and escalation route. Record acceptance and outstanding items.

02 / WORK SAMPLE

Make departures controlled and traceable

Scenario: a manager and HR have confirmed a scheduled departure. Actions follow the organisation’s retention, legal-hold and access policies; this checklist is an operating sample, not legal advice.

Offboarding control record

StageActionEvidence / owner
AuthoriseValidate end time, manager and HR requestApproved ticket / HR
Secure accessDisable sign-in and revoke active sessions as supported; remove remote accessTimestamped admin record / IT
Preserve business workApply approved retention and transfer ownership to an authorised custodianCustodian acceptance / manager
Recover assetsCollect equipment; confirm asset status and return accessoriesAsset receipt / IT
Clean upReview groups, shares, delegated access, SaaS accounts and licencesCompleted checklist / IT
VerifyConfirm access removal and list unresolved exceptionsClosure review / IT and manager

Exception handling

  1. If equipment cannot be recovered, escalate to the device/security owner. Remote wipe requires the appropriate approval and consideration of evidence and retention needs.
  2. If there is a legal hold or unresolved ownership question, preserve data and obtain instructions before deletion.
  3. If sign-in remains possible or an active session persists, keep the ticket open and escalate with evidence.
  4. A shared account or undocumented service dependency is an exception to investigate, not a reason to leave a personal account active indefinitely.

Closure note

“Offboarding completed at the authorised time. Sign-in and remote access were removed, business data ownership was accepted, and asset recovery was recorded. Outstanding items: none in this fictional example. The approval and verification records remain in the restricted service system.”
