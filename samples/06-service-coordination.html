01 / WORK SAMPLE

Respond with empathy and a concrete next step

Scenario: a fictional business client reports a third late service visit in one month. This sample demonstrates transferable service-coordination skills; it is not a claim of employment in cleaning, logistics or account management.

Client email

Subject: Follow-up on the missed service window

Hi Alex,

I’m sorry your team has had to deal with another late visit, especially after raising this before. I understand that the missed window disrupted your plans.

I’m checking the attendance record and speaking with the service lead to confirm what happened and the earliest available recovery option. I’ll update you by 2:00 pm today, even if the final arrangement is still being confirmed.

I’ve also escalated the repeated delays for a review of the schedule and communication process. Once I have the facts, I’ll share the proposed next steps and agree the arrangement with you.

Kind regards,
Toluwa

Why the response works

  1. Acknowledges the repeated impact without arguing with the customer.
  2. Commits to an update time within the coordinator’s control.
  3. Separates investigation from promises that depend on another team.
  4. Escalates the recurring pattern while retaining ownership of client communication.

Internal handoff

Client DEMO-A · Third reported delay this month · Current visit missed the agreed window · Attendance evidence requested · Service lead to confirm cause and recovery capacity · Coordinator retains client updates · Escalate if no recovery proposal is available by 13:30.

Record-keeping discipline

  1. Maintain one case record across email, phone and chat; log a summary and timestamp after each material interaction.
  2. Record what the client requested, what was agreed, who owns the next action and when the next update is due.
  3. Keep private client information in the approved system; share only the minimum needed for the handoff.
  4. Close only after completion is verified and the client has received the outcome. Track recurring causes separately.

Example measures

For an agreed reporting period, distinguish on-time visits / scheduled visits, acknowledgements within target / eligible cases, and reopened cases / closed cases. Define exclusions and timestamps before comparing performance. No invented performance results are claimed here.
