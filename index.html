01 / INFRASTRUCTURE DELIVERY

A connected workplace across three buildings

Harvestfield Industries · via TranterIT · 2025

Designed and delivered a structured enterprise network with wired, wireless and voice services, from site survey through operational handover.

≈120 users / 6 floors

Read the case study

Context

The organisation needed its first structured enterprise network across three two-floor office buildings, with reliable connectivity, central security, wireless access and IP telephony.

My contribution

I handled approximately 95% of technical delivery: requirements, site survey, design, bill of quantities, equipment selection, vendor coordination, cabling supervision, configuration, testing, documentation, user training and handover.

Approach

Implemented the firewall, core/access switching, VLANs, inter-VLAN routing, wireless SSIDs, guest access, VoIP and associated services. Coordinated approximately seven vendors and 15 technicians. When construction activity damaged installed cabling, I met with the client project coordinator to realign contractor schedules and avoid further clashes.

Outcome

Connected approximately 120 users with coverage across six floors, 120 network points, six switches, 12 access points, one firewall and 32 IP phones. The reported project value was ₦15–20 million; core implementation took 6–8 weeks and finished within the agreed budget and timeline.

What this demonstrates

Infrastructure delivery depends on coordination as much as configuration. A clear work sequence and handover plan reduce rework and make the finished environment supportable.
